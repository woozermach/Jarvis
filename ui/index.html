    What Sayro may do on your computer. Switched-off abilities cannot be used.

    A routine is a set of actions you start with one phrase. The easiest way is to say: “Hey Sayro, create a gaming routine: open Steam and Discord, volume 40, quiet mode.” Then: “start the gaming routine”.

    Quiet mode
    In quiet mode Sayro does not start talking on its own — alerts arrive as notifications. Alarms and timers still ring.

    Say “watch my screen” or “follow my game” — Sayro sees your screen for the whole conversation and helps as you go: in games, code or homework (with the Tutor personality — step by step).

    Set the same shortcuts in Discord → Settings → Keybinds (Toggle mute / Toggle deafen).

    Control your PC from Telegram: text or send voice messages — “what's on my screen”, “set a 20-minute timer”, “shut down the PC”. Timers and alerts arrive here too.

    What Sayro remembers about you
    Notes

    “Auto” — Sayro picks the best available model and switches if one is overloaded.

    Your voice, screen frames and messages go straight from your PC to Google Gemini under your own key. Sayro has no servers and does not see your data.

    Your settings, keys and memory are never touched. A copy of the old version is kept, and if the new one fails to start, Sayro rolls back by itself.
