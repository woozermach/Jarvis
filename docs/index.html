Questions

Is Oswin really free?

Yes. Everything except the Pro features is free with no time limit and no ads. Every new install gets 7 days of Pro to try.

What is a Gemini API key and does it cost money?

Oswin's brain is Google Gemini. You connect it with your own key from Google AI Studio. Google offers a free tier that is enough for everyday use; heavy use or extra privacy (see below) needs a paid Google plan, billed by Google. A Google AI Pro subscription may include monthly Google Cloud credit that can pay for this.

Is my voice recorded or stored?

Oswin has no servers of its own and the developer never receives your voice, screen or messages. While you talk, audio goes directly from your PC to Google to be understood and answered. Notes, tasks and settings stay on your PC. Read the Privacy Policy for every detail.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is it safe?

This warning appears for new apps that are not yet code-signed. Click More info → Run anyway. The full source code is public on GitHub.

How do I activate Pro?

After buying, copy the license key from the Gumroad email, open Oswin → Settings → Pro and paste it. If you reinstall Windows or Oswin, just paste the same key again.

How do I cancel?

Open the Gumroad receipt email or your Gumroad library and click “Manage membership” → Cancel. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then Oswin goes back to Free. Nothing is deleted.

Does it work without internet?

Not yet. Understanding speech and answering needs Google Gemini online. An offline mode is on the roadmap.

Which microphones work?

Any. With headphones you can talk over Oswin and interrupt it; with laptop speakers it pauses the microphone while speaking to avoid echo.
