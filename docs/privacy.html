Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

In short. Oswin runs on your computer and has no servers of its own. The developer does not receive your voice, screen, messages, notes or settings. To understand and answer you, Oswin sends what you say (and, only when you ask, what is on your screen) directly from your PC to Google Gemini using your own Google API key. Everything else stays on your PC.

1. Who is responsible

Oswin is developed by Arsenii Minailov, Žilina, Slovakia (“we”, “the developer”). Contact: github.com/woozermach/Jarvis/issues.

For the processing that happens inside the app on your computer, you are in control: you choose which services to connect and can switch features off or uninstall Oswin at any time. The services listed in section 3 act under their own privacy policies.

2. Data stored on your computer

Oswin keeps its files only in its program folder on your PC:

  • config.json: your settings, your Gemini API key, your Telegram bot token (if you connect Telegram), your Pro license key, your name and city if you enter them.
  • data\: timers, reminders, tasks, notes, things you ask Oswin to remember, routines, the last 40 messages of conversation (used to continue the conversation within 24 hours), the trial start date, update backups.
  • jarvis.log: a technical log for troubleshooting. It may contain short fragments of commands.

This data never leaves your PC unless a feature below needs it. You can view or delete it in Settings (Memory, History) or by uninstalling Oswin and choosing to delete your data.

4. About Google Gemini and your API key

Your Gemini API key belongs to you, and Google processes your requests under the Gemini API Additional Terms and the Google Privacy Policy. Under those terms, content sent through the unpaid tier may be used by Google to improve its products and may be read by human reviewers, and Google advises not to submit sensitive or personal information there. For the paid tier (a Google Cloud project with billing enabled) Google states that it does not use prompts and responses to improve its products. According to Google's terms, users in the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the United Kingdom get the paid-tier data rules for all services. If you handle sensitive information on screen or by voice, we recommend enabling billing on your Google project.

5. Purchases

Pro subscriptions are sold by Gumroad, Inc., which acts as the merchant of record and processes your payment details under the Gumroad Privacy Policy. We never see your card details. Gumroad shares with us the information needed to provide your purchase and support you, such as your email address, country and subscription status. We use it only for that, for legally required accounting, and to answer your messages. We keep it as long as tax law requires.

6. Legal basis (EU/EEA/UK users)

  • Providing the features you use and your subscription: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Accounting and tax records of purchases: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Update checks and security fixes: our legitimate interest in keeping the software working and safe (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Optional features (Telegram, screen sharing): your choice to switch them on, which you can undo at any time.

7. Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of personal data we hold about you (in practice, only your purchase information), object to processing, or ask for a copy. Data stored by the app is on your own computer and fully under your control. For data held by Google, Telegram or Gumroad, use their privacy tools. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority; in Slovakia this is the Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR.

8. Children

Oswin is intended for people aged 18 and over, because Google's Gemini API requires its users to be adults.

9. Security

Connections to the services above use HTTPS. Oswin's local web interface listens only on your own computer (127.0.0.1). Your keys are stored in plain text in config.json in your user folder, so keep your Windows account protected with a password.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and describe important changes in the app's “What's new”.
